# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Bot handles data.

1. **Information Collected**
   - Discord user IDs and server IDs for command execution.
   - Basic usage logs (e.g., command names, timestamps).

2. **Information Not Collected**
   - The Bot does not collect messages, passwords, payment information, or personal details.

3. **Data Usage**
   - Data is used only for bot functionality and moderation features.
   - No data is shared with third parties.

4. **Data Retention**
   - Temporary data may be stored in memory or logs for debugging.
   - You can request deletion of stored data by contacting the Bot owner.

5. **Security**
   - Reasonable measures are taken to protect data from unauthorized access.

6. **Updates**
   - This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the Bot means you accept the updated policy.
